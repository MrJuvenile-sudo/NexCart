# Shipping & Returns

**Shipping Policy**
- Free shipping on orders over ₹999.
- Express delivery (24‑hr) available for select locations.
- Standard delivery (3‑5 days) for all regions.

**Returns & Refunds**
- 7‑day return window from delivery date.
- Items must be in original condition and packaging.
- Refunds processed within 5 business days to original payment method.

For detailed terms, please contact our support team.
